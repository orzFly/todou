todou v0.6.1

A Codex conversation can follow the tracker: --follow=codex delivers
comments, spec reviews and answers into it, from the App or a plain
codex TUI alike. Mermaid fences draw as diagrams, and a question card
points at the first unanswered question and can skip the rest. Spec
review drafts no longer vanish across tabs or after an upgrade.

No migration, no configuration change, API untouched — either CLI pairs
with either server.

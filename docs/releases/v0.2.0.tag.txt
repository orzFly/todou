todou v0.2.0

Cross-project work: a global Inbox, one event stream per user, and a
todou watch that spans projects and idles on pushed events instead of
polling. Harness detection now covers claude-code, codex, pi and
hermes-agent, and every part reports its own version.

Two defaults moved: updated_at now tracks activity (comments, attachments,
answered questions, spec pushes and reviews), and todou watch filters out
your own agent session rather than your whole account. Nothing breaking.
